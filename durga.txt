Biggest Risk

The biggest risk is not technology.

It's trust.

Imagine AI says:

"Your girlfriend may be losing interest."

And AI is wrong.

That can create a fight.

So the app should never make strong claims.

Bad:

❌ "She is cheating."

❌ "He doesn't love you."

Good:

✅ "Communication frequency has decreased."

✅ "You may benefit from discussing recent changes."

Stick to observations, not accusations.
